# Behavior cloning with ACT

Train ACT on successful demonstrations and retain a separate episode for checking predictions. Match camera names, state field order, action units and control frequency before combining recordings. The trajectory trainer writes a final.pt checkpoint containing the network and normalization statistics.

[ACT training procedure](https://github.com/JaimeParker/real-world-rl/blob/main/docs/tutorial/09-base-policy.md)
